Keynote Speakers

The 2026 ON-EAR Research Day will feature two keynote presentations.

RISING STAR KEYNOTE

Dr. Lauren Fink

Tracking Auditory Attention Across Time, People, and Places

In this talk, Dr. Fink will trace the development of her research from early laboratory-based pupillometry studies of fluctuations in attention during music listening to current studies in which more than 30 people are eye-tracked simultaneously during live concerts, film screenings, and audiovisual art exhibitions. She will discuss what these approaches can reveal about attention during real-world listening, as well as the methodological challenges of studying auditory experience across participants, social environments, and longer timescales.

DISTINGUISHED KEYNOTE

Dr. Susan Scollie

Innovations in Audiology: Hearing Research in Action
