About ON-EAR

ON-EAR brings together auditory scientists, clinicians, and trainees from across Ontario to share discoveries, build connections, and advance research.

Building on the Toronto Auditory Research Group and organized by the Toronto Metropolitan Auditory Research Cluster (TMARC), ON-EAR creates space for new ideas and meaningful scientific exchange.
