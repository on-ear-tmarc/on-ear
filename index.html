About ON-EAR

ON-EAR connects and showcases auditory research across Ontario. Building on the Toronto Auditory Research Group and organized by the Toronto Metropolitan Auditory Research Cluster, the network supports collaboration, knowledge exchange, and community among auditory scientists, clinicians, and trainees.
